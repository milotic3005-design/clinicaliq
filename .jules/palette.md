## 2024-05-19 - Adding accessible clear buttons to inputs
**Learning:** When implementing a clear button for an input, it's crucial to restore focus to the input, clear autocomplete/suggestion states, use type='button' to prevent form submission, and include a descriptive aria-label and focus-visible styles for keyboard accessibility.
**Action:** Always follow these steps when adding clear buttons to interactive inputs to ensure a fully accessible and seamless UX.
